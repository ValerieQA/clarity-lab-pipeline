# Clarity Lab — Image Generation Prompt

## Master Image Rules

Create a luxury editorial photograph for a reflective article titled "{title}".

The visual scene must be inspired by the article theme: "{core_observation}".

Visual journey state: {visual_state_name}.
Mood: {visual_state_mood}.
Palette direction: {visual_state_palette}.
Optional accent: {accent_state}.

No people. No faces. No silhouettes. No body parts.
Use symbolic objects, architecture, natural forms, light, shadow, texture, space.

Style: high-end editorial photography, luxury magazine aesthetic, refined minimalism.
Lighting: natural light with strong depth and contrast. Deep shadows welcome.
Not blurry. Not painterly. Not illustration. Not CGI.
Looks like Architectural Digest, Kinfolk, or luxury editorial publication.

Square format.
No text. No logos. No letters. No symbols.
No AI-generated logos, no fake letters, no monograms.
