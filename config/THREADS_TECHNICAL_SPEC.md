
# Clarity Lab — Threads Discovery Engine

## Purpose

Threads is not primarily a distribution channel.

Threads is a research and discovery system.

Its purpose is to identify:

* recurring human tensions
* recurring questions
* language patterns
* emotional patterns
* emerging concerns
* future article opportunities
* future product opportunities

The goal is not follower growth.

The goal is not viral reach.

The goal is not direct lead generation.

Those outcomes are considered secondary.

The primary objective is discovery.

# Content Sources

Threads content may be generated from:

Priority Order:

1. Existing Clarity Lab articles
2. Existing Clarity Lab content library
3. Theme database insights
4. Comment analysis findings
5. Recurring audience tensions
6. Trending discussions
7. Administrator-provided topics

The system must never copy article text directly.

It should generate new observations from the same conceptual territory.

---

↓

## Phase 2

Optional

Not part of initial implementation.

# Posting Frequency

Initial frequency:

3 posts per day

Schedule must be configurable.

After sufficient data collection:

* 3 posts/day
* 5 posts/day
* adaptive frequency

The system should support future optimization based on engagement and insight quality.

Do not assume more posts automatically produce better results.

# Duplicate Detection

Before approval or publication:

Compare generated content against:

* recent published posts
* recent drafts
* recent article-derived content

Perform semantic similarity analysis.

If similarity exceeds threshold:

* regenerate content
* flag for review

Goal:

Prevent repetitive observations and conceptual duplication.

# Weighted Comment Analysis

Not all comments have equal value.

Assign weighting.

Examples:

Short agreement:

"I agree."

Weight: 1

Recognition:

"That's exactly what happened to me."

Weight: 5

Detailed personal experience:

Multiple sentences describing a real situation.

Weight: 10

Long-form responses should influence theme detection more heavily.

# Strategic Rule

Threads is not a content factory.

Threads is not an audience growth machine.

Threads is not a lead generation funnel.

Threads is a discovery engine.

The system exists to observe how people experience:

* uncertainty
* work
* relationships
* identity
* information
* AI
* decisions
* change

The purpose is to identify recurring structures in human experience and feed those insights back into the Clarity Lab ecosystem.
