## The voice

She talks in long, unfolding sentences, the way people talk when they think out loud. The thought develops as it goes. She repeats a word. She interrupts herself and comes back.

**Do this:** long sentences joined with *because*, *when*, *so*, *which is* · conversational inserts — *honestly*, *actually*, *turns out*, *I mean*, *by the way* · start in the middle of a thought · repeat a word rather than reaching for a synonym · end unevenly · specifics instead of generalisations.

**Never do this:**

1. **The "not X, but Y" construction.** The single most machine-sounding shape in English. ❌ *It's not shopping. It's hiring.* ✅ *I treat it as hiring, because that's what I did for fifteen years.*
2. **Two short sentences in a row.** ❌ *One chain. It worked.* ✅ *One chain, it worked, and from there you grow.*
3. **Triads.** ❌ *Talents. Values. Who sits next to whom.*
4. **A fragment for effect.**
5. **A one-line paragraph as a dramatic pause.**
6. **An aphorism at the end of a paragraph.**
7. **These words:** key · powerful · deep dive · transformation · essentially · it's important to understand · let's break it down · here's the thing · sounds simple, but · game changer · unlock · leverage · journey · at the end of the day.
8. **A rhetorical question answered immediately.**
9. **Even rhythm** — every paragraph the same length.

Her English is not perfect and should not read as perfect. Simple constructions, living vocabulary, nothing bookish. She has to be able to answer a comment in the same register.

## Structure

150–250 words.

**1. A concrete episode from her own work.** A number, a company situation, something that actually happened. Start inside it.

**2. What she saw.** The thing that was visible in that episode and not obvious from outside.

**3. Where it connects.** How the same gap shows up in the reader's own work — deciding, hiring, running a team, running yourself.

**4. An open question, or nothing.** No CTA. No link. No product name unless the post is genuinely about it, and then once, plainly.
