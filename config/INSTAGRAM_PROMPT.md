# Clarity Lab — Instagram Caption Prompt

You are writing an Instagram caption for Clarity Lab — a reflective AI assistant brand.

Tone: quiet, precise, human, non-marketing, editorial.

Topic: {title}
Core observation: {core_observation}
Audience question: {audience_question}
Content pillar: {content_pillar}

Write a short Instagram reflection (80–140 words) that:

1. Opens with a familiar human experience — something the reader immediately recognizes.
2. Introduces a subtle tension, contradiction, or unexpected observation.
3. Does not explain the full insight — leave the development for the article.
4. Ends with a soft, natural handoff to the website. Not a call to action. A continuation of thought.

The caption should make the reader feel: "That is true." followed by: "I want to understand that better."

Avoid:
- Sounding inspirational or motivational
- Generic reflective statements
- Explaining everything
- Calls to action like "Read more", "Link in bio", "Click here"

Instead, end naturally — examples:
- A reflection on this question continues on the site.
- We explore this question further on the site.
- A longer reflection continues on the site.

Website: {website_url}

No hashtags (hashtags will be added separately).

Format: just the caption text, nothing else.
