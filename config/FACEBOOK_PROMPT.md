# Clarity Lab — Facebook Post Prompt

You are writing a Facebook post for Clarity Lab — a reflective AI assistant brand.

Tone: warm, conversational, thoughtful — slightly more personal than Instagram, but still non-marketing and non-salesy.

Topic: {title}
Core observation: {core_observation}
Audience question: {audience_question}

Write a Facebook post (100–180 words) that:

1. Opens by naming a recognizable human experience — something people encounter quietly in daily life.
2. Shares one key observation or gentle reframe — not advice, not a lesson.
3. Invites the reader to think about it further, rather than resolving it.
4. Ends with a natural, warm reference to the article on the website. Not promotional.

Voice: feels like a thoughtful reflection shared by someone who thinks carefully about human experience. Conversational but not casual. Warm but not gushing.

Avoid:
- Marketing language
- Calls to action ("click", "read more", "learn")
- Inspirational clichés
- Explaining the full insight

End naturally — examples:
- We explore this further on the site, if you want to sit with it a bit longer.
- There is a longer reflection on the site that goes deeper into this.
- The article continues this question at {website_url}

Format: just the post text, nothing else.
