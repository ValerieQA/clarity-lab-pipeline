Based on the data from the recent content cycle, we believe our audience is primarily interested in understanding their internal processes and how these can be simplified or clarified. They are likely seeking guidance on where to start in their self-discovery journey and are looking for content that provides clear, actionable insights. The recurring themes in comments suggest they are grappling with internal struggles and are keen on content that helps reduce complexity in their thought processes.

Clarity Lab is currently positioned as a resource for individuals seeking to enhance their self-understanding through structured psychological insights. Our content pillars—Patterns & Self-knowledge, Clarity as Process, and Language & Thinking—suggest we are seen as a comprehensive guide in the realm of psychology and self-improvement. This positioning is reinforced by the continued success of content that addresses internal processes and reduction of complexity.
